1. The Trinity

The GizzyFx strategy rests on three pillars working in concert. No single pillar works alone — it is the interaction between all three that makes the system self-funding and self-healing.

●

Prop Firm

Your challenge account. Makes the big wins that fund everything.

●

Exness (Cent)

Your hedge account. Absorbs the Prop wins and heals the losses.

●

Recovery Engine

The math that ties both together. Self-healing slippage.

Core principle: You trade the same pair, same time, opposite direction on both accounts. When the Prop wins, Exness loses (a little). When the Prop loses, Exness wins (a little more). The Exness win always exceeds the Prop loss — by design. That surplus is how you recover.

2. How the Hedge Works

Step‑by‑step

Signal triggers

You identify a high-probability setup on your Prop firm charts (e.g. E8Markets $5k challenge).

Place Exness trade FIRST

Before touching the Prop, you place the opposite trade on Exness (Cent account via MetaApi). The lot size is pre-calculated by the engine.

Place Prop trade

Immediately after Exness is confirmed, you place your Prop trade in the opposite direction.

One wins, one loses

Because they’re opposite, exactly one account wins. The other loses. The engine ensures the winner always pays for the loser — plus a little extra.

Buffer absorbs variance

The 20% buffer absorbs the small difference between expected and actual fills (slippage). The Recovery Engine tracks any slippage debt and heals it on the very next trade.

The direction logic

Prop: BUY → Exness: SELL = Hedged

When Prop BUYS and Exness SELLS the same pair at the same time, a price move up means Prop profits and Exness loses. A move down means Exness profits and Prop loses. Because Exness lot size is engineered to produce a larger dollar result per pip, the Exness side always wins more than the Prop side loses.

4. The Healing Engine (Targeted Slippage Martingale)

Slippage is the difference between the expected fill price and the actual fill price. It happens on every trade. The GizzyFx Recovery Engine doesn’t try to eliminate slippage — it isolates the debt and heals it on the very next trade.

How healing works

Trade 1: The Slippage

Prop wins $100. Exness was expected to lose $9.54 but actually loses $9.90 due to slippage. The slippage debt is $9.90 − $9.54 = $0.36.

Trade 2: The Martingale Heal

The engine adds the $0.36 debt to the base Exness Win Target: $4.77 + $0.36 = $5.13. New lot size: $5.13 ÷ (30 × $0.10) = 1.71 lots.

Trade 2 outcome A: Prop loses

Exness wins $5.13. The $0.36 debt is paid. Engine resets to base lot size (1.59). You’re whole.

Trade 2 outcome B: Prop wins

Exness loses $10.26 (1.71 lots × 60 pips × $0.10). The 20% buffer absorbs the extra loss. Debt grows slightly — next trade heals it again.

Trade 3: The Reset

If Trade 2 was an Exness win, the debt is $0. The target drops back to $4.77, lot size drops back to 1.59. The engine is clean.

Important: The Martingale only adjusts lot size — it never changes direction, never adds positions, and never runs away. It’s a single-trade adjustment that resets immediately on the next Exness win.

Trigger: Auto-detected when $300 Prop Target is met.

Phase 2 math

7. Execution Rules

Exness Trade FIRST

Always place the Exness trade via the MetaApi button, wait for green confirmation, then place the Prop trade manually. This prevents “naked Prop” exposure if your internet lags.

Trade London/NY overlap

Best liquidity is 13:00–16:00 EST. Spreads are tightest, slippage is minimal.

Vary entry times

Don’t enter at the same minute every day. Use times like 08:13, 10:42, 14:05 to avoid prop firm AI detection.

Vary SL pips

Rotate between 28, 35, and 22 pips. Consistent SL patterns trigger compliance reviews.

No pending orders ±30min of red-folder news

Check ForexFactory. High-volatility news can cause massive slippage that breaks the healing model.

Verify MetaApi is live

Before trading, confirm the Live MT5 badge shows green (LIVE), not amber (CONNECTING) or blue (SETUP).

Hard refresh after deployment

Press Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R) to load the latest JS bundle.

10. Risk Management

What protects you

  • 20% buffer absorbs slippage
  • Healing engine resets every Exness win
  • Daily cap prevents compliance breach
  • Phase 2 Mega Shield recovers everything
  • No open drawdown — only closed trades

What can break it

  • Ignoring Exness-first rule
  • Trading into red-folder news
  • Using same SL/entry every day
  • MetaApi disconnection mid-trade
  • Manual lot size override
Bottom line: The GizzyFx engine is mathematically sound. But math doesn’t execute trades — you do. The rules exist because the model assumes you follow them. Break the rules and the math breaks with them.
